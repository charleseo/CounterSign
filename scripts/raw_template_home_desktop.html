How a role gets signed

  1. Step 1

    We open the employer's own page

    Not a reposting, not a Telegram screenshot. If we cannot find the role there, it does not go up.

  2. Step 2

    We copy down only what it says

    Countries, pay and tests, word for word. "Remote" never counts as "open to Nigeria".

  3. Step 3

    We sign it, then check again

    Every record carries the date. After 14 days without a recheck it is flagged, after 30 it comes down.
